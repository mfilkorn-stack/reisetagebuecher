Day 2 – Good Friday, April 18 – Good Friday Procession in Enna

Map

Enna: Enna sits at around 930 m and is known as the "navel of Sicily" – Italy's highest provincial capital, with views across the whole interior of the island. The octagonal Torre di Federico is attributed to Emperor Frederick II.

↗ Learn more

The Good Friday procession: Good Friday is the high point of the year in Enna. The tradition dates back to Spanish rule: thousands of members of the confraternities walk silently through the town in robes and pointed hoods, each brotherhood in its own colours. They carry the urn with the dead Christ and the statue of Our Lady of Sorrows from the cathedral along Via Roma. It is one of the most striking Holy Week processions in Italy.
