Day 5 – Wednesday, April 23 – Syracuse & Ortigia

Map

Syracuse: Founded around 734 BC by Greeks from Corinth, Syracuse was once one of the most powerful cities of the ancient world and home of Archimedes. The Neapolis archaeological park holds the Greek theatre and the Latomia del Paradiso, a vast ancient quarry.

↗ Learn more

Ortigia and the cathedral: The old-town island of Ortigia is linked to the mainland by two bridges. The cathedral is a palimpsest: behind the 1728 Baroque façade stand the Doric columns of the 5th-century-BC temple of Athena – you can still see them in the walls inside. UNESCO World Heritage since 2005.
