Day 3 – Holy Saturday, April 19 – Castel di Tusa & Castelbuono

Map

Castel di Tusa: Right across the island to the north coast. Lunch directly on the pebble beach of Castel di Tusa, with wine from the barrel.

Castelbuono: The little town in the Madonie mountains grew up around the Ventimiglia castle, built from 1316. On Piazza Margherita stands the Matrice Vecchia, the old mother church from the 14th century, with frescoes in its crypt. Castelbuono is also known for its pastries and ice cream.
