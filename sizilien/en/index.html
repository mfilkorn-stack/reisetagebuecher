Travel Diary: Sicily

Route: Catania → Enna → Castelbuono → Etna → Syracuse → Ragusa → Piazza Armerina → Caltagirone, Thu April 17 – Sat April 26, 2025

Easter 2025 in Sicily: from Baroque Catania up to Enna, where thousands of hooded confraternity members walk through the town on Good Friday, to the north coast at Castel di Tusa and Castelbuono, into the lava fields and vineyards of Etna, then south-east to Syracuse, Modica and Ragusa Ibla – and finally to the Roman mosaics of the Villa del Casale and the majolica staircase of Caltagirone.
