Day 5 – Tuesday, January 24 – Monte Albán & Santo Domingo

Map

Monte Albán: In the morning we leave town and drive up the mountain. Around 500 BC the Zapotecs began levelling the summit, some 400 m above the valley, to build their capital – the political centre of the Oaxaca Valley for over 1,000 years. The Gran Plaza is enormous, the 360° view over the three valleys even more so. A UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1987, together with Oaxaca's old town.

The "Danzantes": more than 300 stone slabs with contorted figures. Once read as dancers, today mostly interpreted as defeated, sacrificed captives.

In the site museum: Zapotec funerary urns, often showing deities such as the rain god Cocijo.

Evening: Santo Domingo: Back in town along the Macedonio Alcalá pedestrian street to the Templo de Santo Domingo. Plain and massive outside – built to withstand earthquakes – while inside the Baroque explodes: gilded altars, stucco up to the ceiling.

Dinner: Oaxacan cooking lives on corn, chili and patience: blue-corn tortillas, salsas freshly ground at the table in a molcajete (stone mortar).
