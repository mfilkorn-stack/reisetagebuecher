Day 1 – Friday, January 20 – Arrival: Frankfurt → Mexico City

Map

Departure from Frankfurt: The holiday starts where it should: in the lounge, beer in hand. Almost twelve hours of flying ahead of us, plus seven hours of time difference – this is going to be a long day.

Good spirits on board before the jet lag kicks in.

Somewhere over the far north: snowfields and clouds as far as the eye can see.

Approach to Mexico City: On approach, the night-time sea of lights of one of the largest metro areas in the world – more than 20 million people at around 2,240 m in a former lake basin. The city sits on the bed of Lake Texcoco, where the Aztecs once founded Tenochtitlan.

With our driver from the airport into town – first impression: traffic, even at this hour.

First evening: Drop the bags, head out for a first drink. Jet lag can wait.
