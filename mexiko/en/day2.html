Day 2 – Saturday, January 21 – Chapultepec, Polanco, Condesa & a Barbecue in Lomas

Map

Morning walk along Avenida Chapultepec: Up early, on foot through Roma Norte towards Chapultepec. Street stands sell cut fruit in cups – mango, papaya, pineapple, with lime and Tajín (chili salt). Above it all rise the glass towers along Paseo de la Reforma.

Breakfast to go: fruit cups with chili and lime.

Tláloc monolith: In front of the National Museum of Anthropology stands the rain god Tláloc – a stone giant of around 168 tonnes. It was moved into the city from Coatlinchan in 1964; legend has it that a downpour broke out in the middle of the dry season when it arrived.

Polanco: market and breakfast: On Saturdays a tianguis (street market) sets up in Parque Lincoln in Polanco: mountains of limes, mangos and bananas. Then breakfast in a restaurant with a beautifully carved doorway – chilaquiles with a fried egg, the classic cure after a long night.

Condesa in the afternoon: Parque México is the green heart of Condesa – laid out on a former horse-racing track, which explains the oval shape of the neighbourhood (Hipódromo). The fountain with the water-jug bearer at Foro Lindbergh is a landmark of the park, surrounded by Art Deco houses and busy terraces.

Barbecue in Lomas: In the afternoon, an invitation to friends in Lomas: grill on, carne asada, a long table in the garden. Still the best way to get to know a city is at the table of its people.

To finish, a bar in Roma Norte on Avenida Álvaro Obregón.
