Day 6 – Wednesday, January 25 – Oaxaca: Comida & Mezcal

Map

Comida at a comedor: A quiet day in town. Comida, the main meal, is eaten in the early afternoon in Mexico – we end up in a simple family restaurant: quesadillas, milanesa, salsas in every degree of heat.

Mezcal: After the meal, the owner pours mezcal from a big bottle. Oaxaca produces most of Mexico's mezcal, mostly from the Espadín agave, whose hearts are roasted in earth pits – hence the smoky taste. Served with orange slices and sal de gusano, salt with ground agave worm and chili.

↗ Learn more

Tip: Mezcal is sipped, not shot: in small sips, ideally from a jícara or a small glass. The orange with sal de gusano comes afterwards.
