Travel Diary: Mexico

Route: Mexico City → Oaxaca → Tulum → Chichén Itzá → Campeche → Uxmal → Mérida → Mexico City, Fri January 20 – Mon February 6, 2023

Seventeen days in Mexico in January and February 2023: we start in the capital among mariachis, museums and parks, fly into the mountains to Oaxaca for Monte Albán, mole and mezcal, then switch to the Caribbean in Tulum. By rental car we cross the Yucatán Peninsula – Chichén Itzá, the fortified city of Campeche, Uxmal, cenotes and Mérida – before Xochimilco and the Anthropology Museum round things off.
