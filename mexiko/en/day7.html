Day 7 – Thursday, January 26 – Tacos, Mezcalería & the Zócalo at Night

Map

Tacos: A late taquería: grilled meat topped with onion, coriander and a battery of salsas – from mild to "careful".

At the mezcalería: On to a mezcalería. Besides mezcal: regional craft beer and labels worth remembering.

The Zócalo at night: In the evening Oaxaca's centre becomes a stage: esquites stands, costumed performers, families. Protest tents stand on the Zócalo – plantones, used for decades by groups to make their demands visible. The cathedral and arcades are warmly lit.

Pop culture meets colonial backdrop: a "Predator" poses for kids' photos.

At night the pipas roll in: Oaxaca suffers from chronic water shortages and many homes are supplied by tanker truck.
