Day 12 – Tuesday, January 31 – Campeche: Sea, Colours & Calle 59

Map

Lunch by the sea: A slow day: lunch overlooking the Gulf, then a walk through the San Román neighbourhood. There's a classic car on every corner – including a vocho, the classic VW Beetle, which rolled off the line in Puebla until 2003.

Calle 59: In the evening Calle 59 turns into a pedestrian street with tables outside and string lights overhead. Papel picado, the punched tissue paper, even decorates the windows.

Colours of the flag.

And to finish: probably the quirkiest car in Campeche.
