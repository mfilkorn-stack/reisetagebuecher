Day 10 – Tuesday, November 26 – Boat Trip to Costa da Lagoa

Map

Costa da Lagoa: The settlement on the western shore of Lagoa da Conceição can only be reached by boat or on foot along a trail. By motorboat across the lagoon, swimming stops, then the little waterfall of Costa da Lagoa. With it craft beer from Santa Catarina – the German-descended towns inland brew plenty of it.

Evening on the lagoon: Dinner on a pier over the water as night falls.
