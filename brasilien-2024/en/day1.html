Day 1 – Wednesday/Thursday, November 13/14 – Arrival in Rio: Barra, Ipanema, Copacabana

Map

Arrival and Barra da Tijuca: Departure, overnight flight, arrival in the evening – and straight to a churrascaria. On the first morning Barra da Tijuca: some 18 km of beach in western Rio, the Tijucas islands offshore. G20 posters everywhere – the summit is held in Rio a few days later, on 18–19 November 2024.

Rio hosted the 2024 G20 summit – even the lifeguard posts carry the logo.

Ipanema and Copacabana: On the way back along Leblon and Ipanema: the mosaic pavement of the promenade, with the twin peaks of Dois Irmãos behind. In the evening on Copacabana – beer, sand sculptures, the full programme.
