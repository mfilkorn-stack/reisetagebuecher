Day 11 – Wednesday, November 27 – Campeche Island

Map

Campeche Island: By boat to the small island off Campeche beach, often called "the Caribbean of Florianópolis". As an archaeological site with prehistoric rock carvings it is protected national heritage, and daily visitor numbers are limited. White sand, turquoise water – and curious coatis rummaging under the bushes for food.

Coatis (quatis) live wild on the island – feeding them is forbidden.
