Day 12 – Thursday, November 28 – Blumenau

Map

Blumenau: Day trip into the Itajaí Valley. Blumenau was founded in 1850 by the German pharmacist Hermann Blumenau, and it still shows: half-timbered houses, German names in the cemetery, beer steins. The Parque Vila Germânica has hosted Oktoberfest every year since 1984, one of the largest in the world. The Eisenbahn brewery comes from here too.
