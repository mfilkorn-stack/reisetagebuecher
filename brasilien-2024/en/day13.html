Day 13 – Friday/Saturday, November 29/30 – Back to Rio: Christ, Sambódromo & the Port

Map

Back to Rio: Breakfast, then from Florianópolis via Congonhas to Santos Dumont, the city airport right on Guanabara Bay. The G20 banners are still up.

Christ the Redeemer: By the 1884 rack railway through the Tijuca Forest up Corcovado. At the top the statue of Christ, inaugurated in 1931, 30 m tall on an 8 m pedestal, one of the "New Seven Wonders" since 2007. From here you can see the whole city, as far as the Maracanã.

↗ Learn more

Sambódromo, Saara and the port: In the afternoon back to the Sambódromo, where floats and costumes for Carnival are already being built in the sheds, then through Saara, Rio's largest open-air shopping district, and on to the port: a replica of a Portuguese ship of discovery, the Museum of Tomorrow. On Sunday, December 1, we fly home via Lisbon.
