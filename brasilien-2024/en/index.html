Travel Diary: Brazil 2024

Route: Rio de Janeiro → São Paulo → Florianópolis (with a day trip to Blumenau) → Rio de Janeiro, Wed November 13 – Sun December 1, 2024

Almost three weeks in Brazil in November 2024, just before Rio hosted the G20 summit: beaches from Barra to Copacabana, Lapa and the Selarón Steps, a tour of Rocinha, São Paulo with Beco do Batman and the Football Museum, then a good week on Santa Catarina Island – oysters in Santo Antônio de Lisboa, Lagoa da Conceição, Campeche Island and a detour to German-flavoured Blumenau – before Christ the Redeemer rounds it all off.
