Day 7 – Thursday, November 20 – Tattoo, Centro & Porto Maravilha

Map

A souvenir for life: The night after the derby was long – the Flu fans celebrated in front of their local bar until after midnight. At midday, a souvenir that lasts: Christ the Redeemer as a tattoo on the upper arm.

Centro on a holiday: November 20 is Dia da Consciência Negra, a nationwide holiday since 2024 in memory of Zumbi dos Palmares, leader of the largest quilombo community of escaped slaves. The city centre is packed, bands play on the streets of the old town. In the evening Candelária Church glows in the dusk.

Porto Maravilha: The old port district was completely redeveloped before the 2016 Olympics. Its landmark is Santiago Calatrava's Museum of Tomorrow (2015), whose white ribbed structure juts into the bay like a skeleton. Next to it, an old "Made in Germany" harbour crane recalls the past.
