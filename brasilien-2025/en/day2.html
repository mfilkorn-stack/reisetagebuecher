Day 2 – Friday, November 14 – Beach, Lapa & the Selarón Steps

Map

Beach afternoon: Umbrella, plastic chairs, a caipirinha vendor every few minutes. At the end of the beach locals play football and footvolley in front of Morro do Leme.

Lapa: In the evening to Lapa, the nightlife district. The white arches of the aqueduct, completed in 1750, once carried water into the city – since 1896 the tram to Santa Teresa has run across them. Dinner at a Peruvian place: ceviche and pisco.

↗ Learn more

Selarón Steps: From 1990 the Chilean artist Jorge Selarón turned the 215 steps between Lapa and Santa Teresa into a mosaic of more than 2,000 tiles from over 60 countries – many of them gifts from visitors. He worked on it until his death in 2013.

↗ Learn more

Rio's four big clubs on the tiles: Botafogo, Fluminense, Flamengo, Vasco.

To finish, an Eisenbahn – craft beer founded in 2002 in Blumenau, Brazil's most German city.
