Day 5 – Tuesday, November 18 – Boat Trip to Ilha Grande

Map

Along the Costa Verde: Early in the morning by tour bus from Rio down the Costa Verde, about two hours west. Coffee and coconut overlooking the bay of Mangaratiba.

Ilha Grande: By schooner out into the bay of Ilha Grande. The island is car-free and largely covered by Mata Atlântica, the Atlantic coastal rainforest. Until 1994 a notorious prison stood here, which long kept tourism away – today that is exactly its good fortune. Swimming stops in turquoise water, music and caipirinhas on board; on the way back dark clouds roll over the mountains.

Back in Rio: On the way back a giant Christmas tree lights up the roadside – Advent at 30 degrees. In Leme there is still plenty going on in the evening.
