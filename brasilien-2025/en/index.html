Travel Diary: Brazil 2025

Route: Cologne → Rome → Rio de Janeiro (Copacabana, Lapa, Maracanã, Centro) · day trips to Ilha Grande and Petrópolis, Wed November 12 – Sat November 22, 2025

Ten days in Rio de Janeiro in November 2025: base in Leme at the eastern end of Copacabana, plus Lapa and the Selarón Steps, Fort Copacabana, a Fla-Flu at the Maracanã right among the Fluminense fans, the Black Consciousness Day holiday in Centro and Porto Maravilha – and two day trips: by schooner to Ilha Grande and up to the imperial city of Petrópolis.
