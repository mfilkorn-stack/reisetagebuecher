Day 1 – Sunday/Monday, August 30/31 – Via Hong Kong to Cebu

Map

Getting there: Overnight from Frankfurt to Hong Kong, half a day changing planes in the vast terminal at Chek Lap Kok, then on to Cebu. "Dayon kamo sa Cebu!" – welcome in Cebuano, the language of the Visayas.

Down the south coast: With a driver across the bridges to Cebu City, then some three hours down the south coast to Oslob. On the way: tricycles, motorbikes with sidecars that serve as taxis in the Philippines. In the evening cocktails, coconut and barbecue skewers at a roadside grill.
