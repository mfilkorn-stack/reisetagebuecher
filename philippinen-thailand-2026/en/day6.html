Day 6 – Monday–Wednesday, September 7–9 – Koh Tao: Snorkelling & Island Tour

Map

Quiet days: Snorkelling in the bay – a small blacktip reef shark glides past just below the surface. Then som tam, spicy papaya salad, in an open wooden pavilion looking out to sea, and every evening the sunset from the terrace.

Boat tour around the island: By boat around Koh Tao with snorkelling stops. Past Koh Nang Yuan, three small islands joined by a sandbar at low tide. On the way back, a rainbow over the jungle.
