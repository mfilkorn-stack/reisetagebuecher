Day 3 – Wednesday, September 2 – Whale Sharks in Oslob & On to Moalboal

Map

Whale sharks in Tan-awan: Early in the morning, before the buses arrive, in the haze on the coast at Tan-awan. A few metres from shore, whale sharks – the world's largest fish, over twelve metres long, a harmless plankton feeder. With mask and snorkel they are within arm's reach. The fact that fishermen feed the animals so they stay here is controversial: marine biologists say it alters their migration.

On to Moalboal: Up the coast via Samboan to the west side of the island and Moalboal. Right off the shore at Panagsama the reef drops away steeply – home to a huge sardine run, millions of fish you can snorkel to straight from the beach. In the evening, sunset over the Tañon Strait, with Negros on the other side.
