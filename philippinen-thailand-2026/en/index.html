Travel Diary: Philippines & Thailand 2026

Route: Frankfurt → Hong Kong → Cebu (Oslob, Sumilon, Moalboal, Cebu City) → Singapore → Koh Samui → Koh Tao → Koh Samui, Sun August 30 – Sun September 13, 2026

Two weeks in late summer 2026, two countries: first Cebu island in the Philippines – Sumilon Island, whale sharks in Oslob, sardines and sunsets in Moalboal, a night in Cebu City – then on into the Gulf of Thailand to Koh Tao for snorkelling, wreck diving and slowing down, and finally an evening on Koh Samui.
