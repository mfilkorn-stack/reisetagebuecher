Day 5 – Saturday/Sunday, September 5/6 – To Thailand: Koh Tao

Map

On to Thailand: Night flight from Cebu via Singapore to Koh Samui. The airport there belongs to Bangkok Airways and consists of open, thatched pavilions. By ferry on to Koh Tao – in the evening a Luang Prabang beer on the terrace, the view across the jungle to the sea.

Koh Tao: The "Turtle Island" in the Gulf of Thailand covers only about 21 km² and is one of the places where most people in the world earn their diving certificate. First day: pad thai on Sairee Beach, the bay looking out to Koh Nang Yuan, bars among rocks and driftwood, sunset from the terrace and, in the evening, mu kratha, a tabletop grill with a soup moat.
